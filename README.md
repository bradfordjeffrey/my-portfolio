# Jeffrey Bradford Lamptey — Portfolio

This is my personal portfolio website. It introduces who I am and showcases my experience and skills in IT operations, web development, business and data analytics, and data administration.

Each area has its own page describing what I do, the skills I bring, and projects I have worked on. The site also includes my education, work history, a gallery of my photography and videography, and a contact form so visitors can reach me directly.

**Built with:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Node.js and Express, hosted on Vercel.
